NOWBAT SNAP CAPTURE - DISPLAY COMPATIBILITY

SNAP syntax:
  SNAP "SCREEN.BMP"
  SNAP "SCREEN.BMP" BPP=4
  SNAP "CROP.BMP",0,0,63,31,BPP=1

SNAP selects FAST direct video memory by default. Omitted coordinates
capture the whole desktop; omitted BPP selects desktop-native depth.
FAST supports known 1-bpp and 4-bpp layouts and requires X and the
capture width divisible by 8. Unsupported display modes return RESULT=2.

The compatibility command accepts the same arguments:
  SNAPSAFE "SCREEN.BMP"
  SNAPSAFE "SCREEN.BMP" BPP=4
  SNAPSAFE "CROP.BMP",0,0,63,31,BPP=1

SNAPSAFE always uses the original GDI GetPixel path. This remains the
safe method for unknown video drivers and accurate RGB conversion.

IMPORTANT: Hardware palette accuracy on FAST colour captures may vary,
especially in 800x600 16-colour mode 6Ah under Windows 1. Use SNAPSAFE
when accurate RGB conversion or an unfamiliar display driver is needed.
